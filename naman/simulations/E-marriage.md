# Simulation E — Marriage (scenario definition, not yet run)

Models the effect of marriage or a major lifestyle change between two subjects.

**Tracks:** subject pairing, age at marriage if provided, health status of each
partner, diet, exercise, sleep, stress, shared household habits, medication
adherence, financial/social assumptions only if explicitly supplied, potential
genetic and reproductive-health considerations.

**Guardrail:** no deterministic predictions about marriage outcomes. These are
synthetic test subjects (see `PROJECT-SPEC.md` preamble) — this scenario exists to
exercise the simulator, not to model or rank any real person's marriage prospects.

No pairing has been selected yet — this is the scenario definition only.
